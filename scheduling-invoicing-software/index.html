Scheduling & invoicing software comparisons

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Most field service apps cover the same core jobs: quotes, scheduling and dispatch, invoices, and taking payments. The differences that tend to matter to a small business are in how pricing scales with users, which features sit on higher plans, and what payment processing costs on top of the subscription.

What our comparisons will cover

  • Pricing structure: base plan, per-user charges, and which features require an upgrade, from each vendor's pricing page with the date checked.
  • Payments: published card-processing rates and whether outside processors are supported.
  • Core workflow: quotes, scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, and client communication features as documented by the vendor.
  • Trial and cancellation terms as published.
