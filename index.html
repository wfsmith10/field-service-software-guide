Scheduling & invoicing software for small trade businesses

Field service software (tools such as Jobber, Housecall Pro and their alternatives) handles quotes, scheduling, dispatch, invoicing and payments for trade businesses. Plans, per-user pricing and add-ons differ a lot, and the real monthly cost is often hard to work out from pricing pages.

This site compares them on the things a small crew actually pays for and uses, with every price and feature linked to the vendor's own published page and dated.
