How pages are made

  • Pages are researched and drafted with AI assistance, then checked against the sources they cite before publishing.
  • Facts such as dimensions, prices, plan limits or features come from a cited source, usually the maker's own published material. If we can't source it, we leave it out.
  • We do not claim hands-on testing unless a page explicitly says so and describes what was done.
  • Each page shows a "last updated" date. Details change, so confirm critical facts with the maker before buying.
