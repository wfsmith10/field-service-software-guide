Field service software cost calculator: Jobber vs Housecall Pro

Edited by William Smith · researched with AI assistance · Last updated 2026-11-23 · How we make pages

Short answer: your monthly cost is the plan price for your number of users, plus any extra users, plus payment fees. Enter your numbers below. The calculator uses only the prices on Jobber's and Housecall Pro's own pricing pages, checked 2026-11-23. For most crew sizes the cheapest option on each plan is not obvious. For example, on Jobber Connect a 3-person crew pays less on the 5-user package ($149 billed annually) than on the 1-user package plus two $29 users ($157).

Owners ask for an honest, all-in price because the subscription is only part of the bill. One example is a request for an honest pricing comparison between Jobber and Housecall Pro on r/PressureWashingGrowth. Our Jobber vs Housecall Pro comparison shows 1, 5, 8, 10 and 15 users. This page works out any crew size from 1 to 20, all three Jobber billing options, and your own card and bank volume. We have not used either product. The numbers are arithmetic on published prices, not quotes.

How the calculator works

  • Jobber sells Connect and Grow as 1-, 5-, 10- and 15-user packages and Plus as 5-, 10- and 15-user packages, and says "Add users for $29/mo each." For each plan the calculator tries every package, adds $29 for each user above the package size, and shows the cheapest. Using a smaller package plus extra users is our reading of that note. Ask Jobber to confirm before you rely on it.
  • Jobber Core is listed for 1 user, so it is only priced for 1 user here. Its card also carries the $29 extra-user note, so ask Jobber if you want Core with more people.
  • 16 or more users on Jobber: the team-size menu has a "16 or more" option, and the page shows "Contact Sales" for it. The calculator does not guess that price.
  • Housecall Pro includes 1 user on Basic, 5 on Essentials and 8 on MAX, and lists "$35/mo per additional user" on MAX only. With no extra-user price shown for Basic or Essentials, the calculator marks those plans "not listed" above their included users. It assumes the $35 does not change with billing cycle, since the page does not say.
  • Payment fees: Jobber lists 2.9% + 30¢ for online card payments (2.7% + 30¢ for Tap to Pay) and 1% for bank payments (ACH), plus an extra 1% for instant payouts. Housecall Pro says card fees "start at 2.59%" and that "A 1% fee is applied to bank payments." It lists no per-payment fee, so its card fee is shown as a minimum ("at least"). The calculator uses online card rates, with no Tap to Pay or instant payouts.
  • Left out: sales tax, add-ons (such as Jobber's Receptionist and Pipeline add-ons) and promotions. On 2026-11-23 Housecall Pro's page showed a Black Friday offer of 50% off the first 3 months. The calculator uses regular prices.

What the numbers show

  • Jobber package jumps: on Connect, the 5-user package ($149 annually) costs less than the 1-user package plus extra users from 3 users up ($99 + 2 × $29 = $157). At 6 and 7 users, the 5-user package plus extra users costs less than the 10-user package. At 8 users the 10-user package is cheaper ($229 vs $149 + 3 × $29 = $236).
  • Housecall Pro's step to MAX: Essentials covers up to 5 users. For 6 to 8 users the only plan with a listed price is MAX at $299 a month billed annually, whatever the crew size in that range.
  • Payment fees can be larger than the subscription. On $10,000 a month in 40 online card payments, Jobber's listed rate comes to $302 and Housecall Pro's lowest advertised rate to at least $259. Moving the same amount to bank payments costs $100 on either at the listed 1%.

Before you decide

  1. Count everyone who needs a login, office staff included.
  2. Run the calculator for your crew size now and a year from now.
  3. Check that the features you need are on the plan you priced. Price history matters too: see Jobber's plan prices over time.
  4. Check both pricing pages on the day you buy. Prices change.
